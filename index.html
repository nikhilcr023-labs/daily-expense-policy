1. Introduction

Welcome to Daily Expense: Track&Save, developed and published by nikhilcrlabs ("we", "us", or "our"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the information you process within our application.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application (Package: com.spendsense.xydgqk) collects, uses, handles, and safeguards your information when you use our services, including our ad-supported free tier and premium subscription tiers.

2. Information We Collect

We believe in data minimization. The app only collects and handles data essential for financial tracking functionality:

  • Expense & Transaction Records: Expense amounts, category assignments (e.g., Food, Shopping, Travel, Bills, or custom categories), dates, payment notes, and optional budget limits.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you choose to sign in via Google Sign-In / Firebase Authentication, we store basic profile details (email address and display name) strictly to sync your personal expense entries across your devices.
  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Non-personally identifiable technical details such as operating system version, device model, and anonymous crash telemetry used exclusively to maintain stability and performance.

5. Application Permissions

The app only requests standard Android permissions necessary for its intended capabilities:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for loading AdMob advertisements, cloud backup synchronization, and Google authentication.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify active internet connectivity before attempting network operations.

Note: We do not request sensitive permissions such as location tracking, camera, microphone, contacts, or broad external media storage.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Local Database: By default, all your transaction logs, categories, and preferences are stored securely on your local device using Android Room SQLite database architecture.
  • Cloud Synchronization: When authenticated via Firebase, your records are transmitted and stored securely using TLS/HTTPS encryption standards.
  • Data Retention & Deletion: You retain complete control over your data. You can delete individual records, clear categories, or request full account and cloud record deletion by contacting our support email.

7. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal identifiable information from children under 13. If we become aware that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we will take immediate steps to delete such data from our records.
