1. Introduction & Overview

Welcome to Daily Expense: Track&Save ("the App"), developed and published by nikhilcrlabs ("we", "us", or "our"). We are committed to respecting your privacy, protecting your personal financial information, and maintaining full transparency in accordance with the Google Play Developer Program Policies and applicable global data protection regulations.

This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application (Package Name: com.spendsense.xydgqk) handles user data across both our ad-supported Free tier and our premium Pro Subscription tiers.

2. Information We Handle & Collect

Zero Mandatory Account Registration: No phone number, OTP, email address, password, or sign-in is required to use Daily Expense: Track&Save. All your financial logs remain stored locally on your device.

We believe in strict data minimization. The types of data handled by the App include:

  • Financial & Expense Records: Transaction amounts, custom category labels (e.g., Food, Travel, Shopping, Utilities, Entertainment, or user-created categories), dates, notes, and monthly budget limits entered by you. All transaction and financial records are stored 100% locally on your device in a sandboxed SQLite (Room) database. We never transmit, upload, or sell your financial data to any external server.
  • Device & Diagnostics Data: Non-personally identifiable technical telemetry (such as device model, OS version, and crash diagnostics) may be collected automatically by Google Play Services to maintain app stability and performance.
  • User-Generated Backups: If you export your transactions to CSV or generate a JSON backup, the resulting files are created directly on your local device storage under your explicit control.

To provide and maintain the Free tier, our app integrates the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK. Google AdMob and its advertising technology partners may collect and use information to deliver relevant, personalized, or non-personalized advertisements adhering to Google Play policies.

AdMob may collect:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID), which you can reset or delete at any time in your Android system settings (Settings → Google → Ads).
  • Diagnostic, performance, and ad interaction metrics to combat ad fraud and ensure proper ad serving.

To learn more about how Google processes information for advertising, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

100% Ad-Free Pro Tier: Users who subscribe to the Pro Plan enjoy a completely ad-free experience. When a Pro subscription is active, all AdMob ad banners, interstitials, and ad-tracking requests are disabled.

5. Application Permissions

The App complies with the principle of least privilege and requests only standard install-time permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for loading AdMob advertisements (on the Free tier) and verifying Google Play subscription status.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Used to verify active network connectivity before querying Google Play or loading ads.

Zero Sensitive Permissions: We do NOT request access to sensitive hardware or personal data. We do not access your device location (GPS), camera, microphone, contacts, phone calls, SMS messages, or broad media storage. User-initiated file exports (CSV/JSON) are handled safely via Android's modern Storage Access Framework and FileProvider.

6. Data Storage, Security & Encryption

  • Local Storage: All user financial entries, budget configurations, and categories are saved in Android's sandboxed local application directory on your physical device, inaccessible to other applications.
  • Encryption in Transit: Any necessary network communication (such as communication with Google AdMob and Google Play Billing APIs) is strictly encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).

7. User Rights & Data Deletion (Google Play Data Safety)

You have absolute ownership and control over your financial data. We provide several easy methods to manage, export, and delete your data:

  • In-App Deletion: You can edit or delete individual transactions and custom categories directly from the Ledger and Category screens at any time.
  • Export Data: You can export all your financial records into a standard CSV file at any time from the Settings screen.
  • Complete Data Purge: You can permanently wipe all stored data by going to your Android device's Settings → Apps → Daily Expense: Track&Save → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application. This immediately and irreversibly deletes all local databases and preferences.
  • Data Deletion Requests: Because we do not operate user accounts or external servers that store your financial logs, your data exists exclusively on your device. For questions or guidance on data deletion, you may also email us at nikhilcr023@gmail.com.
